Who we are

Small actions. Lasting impact.

GREEN WAVE is an environmental and waste-management initiative committed to promoting a cleaner, greener and healthier environment, starting from Jalingo Metropolitan Area, Taraba State, Nigeria.

♻

Our Mission

Promote proper waste disposal, recycling, environmental sanitation and sustainable waste-management practices.

🌍

Our Vision

A clean, healthy and environmentally responsible community where people work together to protect their surroundings.

🤝

Our Approach

Build awareness, encourage community participation and support practical environmental action.

What we do

Turning concern into action.

Our work focuses on practical steps that help communities manage waste responsibly.

♻️

Recycling Awareness

Encouraging the recovery and reuse of recyclable materials.

🧹

Environmental Sanitation

Promoting cleaner streets, public spaces and neighbourhoods.

📣

Community Education

Sharing useful information on responsible waste handling.

🌱

Community Participation

Connecting residents and volunteers to local environmental action.
